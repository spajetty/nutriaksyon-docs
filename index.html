What NutriAksyon does

NutriAksyon is a web-based school nutrition monitoring and feeding program management system.

Schools already collect height and weight every year, but the results usually stay on paper. NutriAksyon keeps each measurement tied to a learner and a school year, computes the nutritional status right away, and shows whether a learner is improving across feeding cycles.

The same data drives the feeding program. Coordinators plan menus, schedule feeding sessions per section, and record attendance, so a menu is never planned separately from the learners it is meant for.

  • Measurements are recorded once and reused across reports
  • Nutritional status is computed, not estimated
  • Menus and feeding sessions are linked to real sections
  • Every role sees only the data it needs

User roles at a glance

Four access levels, each with a different scope of data.

ROLEMAIN RESPONSIBILITYACCESS SCOPE
TeacherImports the class list, records height and weight, follows each learner's BMI and HFA trend, and reports status and attendance.Own class only
Feeding CoordinatorSchedules feeding sessions and growth monitoring, plans meals from available crops, and exports school-wide reports.Whole school
AdminCreates and revokes teacher and coordinator accounts, maintains grade levels and sections, and manages the school year.Whole school
SuperAdminIssues the sign-up tokens that admins need, so new admin accounts cannot be created without approval.Accounts only
